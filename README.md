AI & WORKFORCE ANALYTICS
Excel Dashboard Project Report
1. Introduction
AI and workforce analytics have become important tools for understanding how artificial intelligence is influencing employees, job roles, salaries, work patterns, and skill development. Organizations can use workforce data to identify salary trends, analyze AI adoption, understand remote-working patterns, and determine the skills employees may need to develop in the future.
This project, titled “AI & Workforce Analytics,” presents an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze workforce-related data using different parameters such as experience level, salary currency, AI tool usage, job roles, team size, remote work ratio, and interviews-to-offer information.
The dashboard converts raw workforce data into meaningful visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), making it easier to understand important trends and support data-driven decision-making.

2. Objectives of the Project
The main objectives of this project are:
1.	To analyze average and highest salaries across the workforce.
2.	To examine the relationship between job roles and AI tool usage.
3.	To analyze employee experience levels and workforce characteristics.
4.	To understand remote-working patterns across different industries.
5.	To analyze team sizes associated with different job roles.
6.	To examine interviews-to-offer trends across different salary currencies.
7.	To understand the importance of upskilling in an AI-driven workplace.
8.	To create an interactive and visually appealing Excel dashboard for workforce analysis.
9.	To present complex workforce data in a simple and understandable format.

The project was developed using Microsoft Excel and its data analysis and visualization features.
The major Excel features used include:
•	Data cleaning and organization
•	Pivot Tables
•	Pivot Charts
•	Dashboard design

5. Key Dashboard Components
5.1 Average Salary
The dashboard shows an average salary of 89,362.06. This provides an overall indication of the earning level represented in the dataset.
Salary can vary significantly depending on factors such as experience, job position, industry, location, and technical skills. Therefore, the average salary serves as a useful starting point for comparing workforce groups.
5.2 Average Weekly Working Hours
The average weekly working time shown in the dashboard is 45.23 hours.
This metric helps in understanding the general workload represented in the dataset. It can also be compared with different job roles and industries to identify areas where employees may be working longer hours.
5.3 Average AI Usage
The dashboard reports an average AI usage score of 6.91.
This indicates that AI tools have a meaningful presence in the workforce represented by the dataset. The AI usage chart also demonstrates that adoption differs considerably between job roles.
5.4 Highest Salary
The highest salary displayed is 276,910.
The difference between the average salary and highest salary demonstrates that compensation can vary substantially across job positions and levels of expertise.
5.5 Average Upskill Score
The dashboard shows an average upskill score of 9.51.
Upskilling is particularly important in an environment where AI and automation are changing the nature of jobs. A high focus on skill development can help employees adapt to changing workplace requirements.

2.	Employees work an average of approximately 45.23 hours per week.
3.	AI usage varies significantly between different job roles.
4.	AI-related positions generally show stronger AI usage compared with some traditional analytical roles.
5.	Remote-working patterns differ across industries.
6.	Team sizes vary considerably depending on job position.
7.	Recruitment activity differs across salary currencies, with the USD category showing the highest value in the Interviews-to-Offer chart.
8.	Upskilling is an important workforce requirement in an AI-driven environment.
9.	Workforce analytics can help organizations understand salary, recruitment, work arrangements, AI adoption, and skill development together rather than analyzing them separately.
